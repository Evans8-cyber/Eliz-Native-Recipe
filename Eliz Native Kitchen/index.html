Our Story

Three pots. One country's worth of flavour.

Eliz Native Kitchen started with a simple question: why should you have to choose between the peppery clarity of an Igbo Ofe Nsala, the deep vegetable richness of a Yoruba Efo Riro, and the smoky palm-fruit depth of an Edo Banga Soup?

So we didn't choose. We learned all three the proper way — from women who have cooked them for decades — and we cook them fresh, every single day, in small pots, the way it's meant to be done.

Igbo

South East

Bitterleaf, uziza, ugu and cocoyam — soups built for pounded yam and fufu.

Yoruba

South West

Efo, locust bean and gbegiri — layered pots eaten with amala and eba.

Edo

South South

Banga spice, owo and native pepper — soups from the heart of Benin.
